# Reference Visuals: Owner Notes and What We Take From Them

Source: owner's notes on five YouTube references (2026-11-04). Used to tune Stage 2 slices 2.4 (tunnel) and 2.5 (particles). The videos themselves were not analysed by the agent; the notes are the owner's description.

## What the five share
- **Forward motion is steady.** Camera flies straight in or along a path with no shake. Beats move lights and shapes, not the camera.
- **Dark base.** Most of the screen is deep black, navy or slate. Colour comes from thin lines, light bands and small points.
- **Beat acts on structure.** The beat flashes bands on pillars, thickens ring outlines, or lights a few shapes. It doesn't flood the whole screen.
- **Sparkle is sparse and directional.** Dust, sparks or pinpricks, often streaming outward from the vanishing point.
- **Intensity varies by section.** Quiet parts are restrained; only drops and climaxes get bright or busy.

## What this changes in the plan
1. **Tunnel (slice 2.4):** flight speed stays steady, with a small gentle change from energy. No camera shake on any beat. Beat drives ring stroke brightness and thickness.
2. **Particles (slice 2.5):** dust moves **outward from the vanishing point**, like the Neon Particle Tunnel. Density is sparse in quiet parts and rises only in louder sections.
3. **Palette:** a palette uniform with a dark base. Default: cyan, magenta, ultraviolet on near-black, as in the Neon Tunnel. Alternatives: amber/emerald and crimson/coral, both from the references.
4. **Structure beats:** beats brighten thin rings or lines, never the full screen.
5. **Strobe limit:** the Zedd drop is out of scope. The safety pass (slice 2.2) keeps flashes within WCAG 2.3.1 limits even when the music is intense.
